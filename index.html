01 About Me

Mechanical Engineer · Aerospace Enthusiast

Currently pursuing my Bachelor of Engineering (Honours) in Mechanical Engineering at Monash University, Melbourne, through the Monash-Mahindra 2+2 Dual Degree Program, following two years at Mahindra University, Hyderabad, with a CGPA of 9.5. My passion lies at the intersection of aerospace systems, computational fluid dynamics, and autonomous robotics.

I currently work on an automated robotic product-processing line at MMAPT Robotics, and specialize in FEA, CFD analysis, turbulence modeling, fluid-structure interaction and perception-driven control, with extensive experience in ANSYS Fluent, ANSYS Mechanical, OpenFAST, OpenFOAM, and ROS2.

My research interests include wall-bounded turbulence, adverse pressure gradient boundary layers and separation, data-driven models of wall shear stress, and high-fidelity simulation at scale.

Aerodynamics Research Published in ICSV32 · Mar 2026

Vortex-induced Vibrations in 15MW Offshore Wind Turbine Blades

S. Kumawat, N. Datta - International Congress on Sound and Vibration (ICSV32), Istanbul, Turkey

  • Accepted on the first attempt with unanimous reviewer scores of 10/10.
  • Designed an end-to-end aeroelastic-CFD-ML pipeline using dynamic meshing with k-ω SST turbulence closure (URANS).
  • Coupled high-fidelity CFD with a Gaussian Process Regressor reconstructing blade stress from sparse sensor data at ~0.8% RMSE.
  • Derived a pitch control strategy that reduced peak flap-wise stresses by ~20%.
  • Ran tuning-factor (λ) resonance analysis to identify critical aeroelastic lock-in regimes.
